WiZdom Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WiZdom ("WiZdom", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects personal information when you use the WiZdom student mobile application (the "App") and the related WiZdom services and website (together, the "Service").

WiZdom is a study-abroad guidance service. The App is used by students who have an account created for them by a WiZdom counsellor or through WiZdom's enquiry process. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Policy.

1. Information we collect

a. Account and profile information

When a WiZdom counsellor creates your account, or when your account is created from a WiZdom enquiry, we hold:

  • Your name
  • Your email address (used to sign in and to send account emails)
  • Your phone number
  • Your password, stored only in irreversibly hashed form
  • Study-related profile details such as gender, country/countries of interest, intended intake month and year, and course of interest
  • Service-management details such as your assigned counsellor's name, a batch/group label, your account status, and your payment status
  • A profile photo, if a WiZdom staff member adds one for your account
  • The date and time you last signed in
  • A count of recent failed sign-in attempts and any temporary lockout time, used only to protect your account

b. Student Information Form (SIF) data

The App includes a Student Information Form that you fill in to help WiZdom prepare your applications, Letter(s) of Recommendation (LOR), and Statement of Purpose (SOP). Depending on what you choose to enter, this can include:

  • Personal and passport details: date of birth, address, whether you have had a previous visa rejection and its details, emergency contact name and relationship, marital status and (if applicable) spouse details, passport number, and passport issue/expiry dates
  • Academic history: your qualifications, subjects, year of passing, marks/percentage, number of backlogs, and the schools/colleges and boards/universities attended
  • Work and internship experience: employer name and address, your designation, monthly salary, and dates
  • Document checklist: which application documents you already have ready
  • Statement of Purpose notes: course name, your motivation, expectations, future plans, and any additional information you provide
  • Letter of Recommendation details: information about each recommender you list, including their name, official phone number, official email address, the degree they studied, their CGPA, and notes about the subjects, projects, and activities they can speak to. You should only provide a recommender's contact details if you are permitted to share them.

Some study-preference answers (for example preferred countries and streams, budget, preferred intake, how you heard about WiZdom, and entrance-test / admission-support preferences) may be carried over from an enquiry form you previously submitted to WiZdom and shown to you in the App.

c. Chat messages

The App has a group chat between you and the WiZdom team. We store the messages you send and receive, including their text, the sender, and timestamps, along with read/delivery status and whether a message was edited or deleted. Deleted messages are hidden in the App but retained in our records for a limited period as an internal audit trail.

d. Notifications

We generate in-app notifications about your application journey (for example when a stage of your process is updated) and store their title, text, and read status.

e. Device and technical information

  • Push notification token: if you allow notifications, we store the push token(s) issued to your device installation(s) so we can send you alerts.
  • Server logs: like most online services, our servers automatically record technical information when the App communicates with us, including your IP address, the type of request, the date and time, the response status, and your device's browser/HTTP user-agent string. These logs are used for security, debugging, and operating the Service.
  • Session token: after you sign in, an authentication token is stored securely on your device (in the platform's encrypted secure storage on mobile) so you stay signed in. It is not shared with anyone else.

The App does not contain third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not collect advertising identifiers or your device's location.

2. How we use your information

We use the information described above to:

  • Create and manage your WiZdom account and authenticate you
  • Provide the Service: track and display your application journey, prepare your applications, LORs and SOP, and guide you through the study-abroad process
  • Enable communication between you and your WiZdom counsellor and support teams through the in-app chat and, where relevant, email
  • Send you notifications about updates to your process
  • Secure the Service, prevent abuse, and investigate technical problems
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms where necessary

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

3. Push notifications

If you grant notification permission, the App registers a push token with the Expo push service and sends it to our servers. When we need to alert you (for example about a journey update), we send the notification content and your token to the Expo push service, which delivers it through the operating system's push infrastructure (Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android, Apple Push Notification service on iOS). You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings; the rest of the App will continue to work.

4. How your information is shared

We share personal information only as described here:

  • Within WiZdom: WiZdom staff — including your assigned counsellor and the relevant admissions, editing, application, and support teams — can access your account, SIF data, journey, and chat in order to provide the Service.
  • Service providers (sub-processors): we use trusted third parties to run the Service, and share information with them only to the extent needed:
    • a cloud database provider that hosts our database (MongoDB Atlas)
    • the Expo push notification service and the platform push services (Firebase Cloud Messaging / Apple Push Notification service) to deliver notifications
    • an email delivery provider, used to send account emails (such as your welcome email and password-reset email) to your email address
    • our application hosting/infrastructure provider(s) that run the WiZdom servers
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of WiZdom, our users, or others.
  • Business transfers: if WiZdom is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Policy.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

5. Where and how your information is stored

Your information is stored in a cloud-hosted database operated on our behalf by our database provider. Files that WiZdom staff upload for your account (such as a profile photo) are stored on WiZdom's server storage. All communication between the App and our servers takes place over encrypted connections (HTTPS/WSS).

Our service providers may store or process data on servers located in countries other than your own. Where information is transferred internationally, we take steps intended to ensure it remains protected consistently with this Policy.

6. How we protect your information

  • Passwords are stored only as salted, irreversible hashes.
  • Access to the App's data requires a signed authentication token; tokens expire and can be invalidated.
  • Repeated failed sign-in attempts trigger a temporary lockout to resist password-guessing.
  • All network traffic is encrypted in transit.
  • On your device, your session token is kept in the operating system's secure storage.
  • Access to student information within WiZdom is limited to staff who need it to provide the Service.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data retention

We keep your personal information for as long as your WiZdom account is active and for as long as needed to provide the Service. We may retain certain information for a longer period where necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, maintain security and audit records, or enforce our agreements. When information is no longer needed, we delete it or remove its identifying details.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: your core profile details are managed by your WiZdom counsellor — contact them or us to review or correct them. You can view and update your Student Information Form directly in the App.
  • Password: you can reset your own password from the Profile screen in the App.
  • Notifications: you can disable push notifications in your device settings at any time.
  • Deletion: you can request deletion of your account and associated data (see the next section).

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable data-protection laws, such as the right to object to or restrict certain processing, or to request a copy of your data. To exercise any right, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

9. Account and data deletion

The App does not currently include a self-service "delete account" button. To delete your WiZdom account and the personal information associated with it, contact your counsellor or email us at the address in the "Contact us" section and ask for your account to be deleted. We will action verified requests within a reasonable time, except for information we are required or permitted by law to retain (for example, limited records kept for legal, security, or audit purposes).

10. Children's privacy

The Service is intended for prospective higher-education students. It is not directed to, and is not intended for use by, children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are under 18, you should use WiZdom only with the involvement of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

11. Third-party links and portals

The App links to external websites and portals that are not operated by WiZdom — for example a course-guidance website and university or visa portals. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and this Policy does not apply to them. We encourage you to review the privacy practices of any third-party site you visit.

12. Device permissions

The App may ask for the following device permissions:

  • Notifications: to deliver alerts about your application journey. Optional — the App works without it.

The App does not use your device's camera, microphone, location, photos, or files. You can review and change permissions at any time in your device settings.

13. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page, and, where appropriate, notify you in the App. Your continued use of the Service after an update means you accept the revised Policy.
